Our method

We teach the essence, not just the shapes.

Our classes deconstruct the most subtle and interesting parts of the dance, and are structured to be equally valuable to leaders and followers alike. The more you dance, the faster it clicks — so we make room for real practice, and plenty of laughter along the way.

Buenos Aires tango holidays
