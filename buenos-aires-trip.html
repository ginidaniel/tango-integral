Tango holidays

The heart of tango, with people who know it.

Eight days in Buenos Aires at the end of the Argentine summer — exploring tango and the magic of this city with those who know it best. A journey you will never forget.

20–27 March 2027

Why come with us

Buenos Aires from the inside.

Anyone can book a flight. What's hard to book is the part that only comes from knowing the city — which milonga is worth your Tuesday, which teacher will change how you dance, which barrio you want to wake up in.

Very limited spaces. We want to build great relationships with our guests and tailor the experience to make it truly unique — so the group stays small.

  • 01

    People who live it

    Every one of our team was born in Buenos Aires, has lived there, or knows it well. The way to really know the soul of a city is to know its people.

  • 02

    Planned by specialists

    We have specialists in tourism working to make your journey as smooth and as well-informed as possible.

  • 03

    A barrio, not a hotel strip

    You stay in a beautiful, historic neighbourhood, with most of what you need and want already on your doorstep.

  • 04

    And above all — where to dance

    The best, most authentic milongas, inspirational classes, and we'll show you where to find all your dancing essentials.

What's included

Everything but the flight.

  • Accommodation in exclusive apartments in a typical San Telmo house, breakfast included*
  • The best combination of milongas, carefully chosen from the heart of the tango scene
  • Welcome dinner at a traditional bodegón
  • Group classes at the Tango Integral school in Buenos Aires
  • Workshops with guest master teachers — world-class maestros
  • Tango shopping tour
  • City tour, including La Boca
  • Bike tour through the ecological reserve and the city's famous parks — or a day at a country house with a swimming pool
  • A daytime tour with a proper picnic, visiting one of the most famous and entertaining museums in Buenos Aires
  • Dinner at one of the best restaurants in Buenos Aires — or in the world
  • Farewell party: a traditional homemade Argentine asado
  • Transfer from the airport on arrival†

* Pictures and details of the apartments at bairescolonial.com.ar.

† The transfer back to the airport at the end of the trip is not included.
