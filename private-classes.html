Private lessons

The quickest way to learn.

Sixty minutes, entirely yours, at a time that suits you — and the strongest possible complement to the group class.

Why a private.

  • 01

    Tailored to you

    The hour goes on whatever is actually holding your dancing back, which is rarely what you assumed it was.

  • 02

    At your own pace

    Take your dancing to the next level without waiting for a group to arrive at the same question.

  • 03

    Flexible times

    Arranged directly with your teacher, rather than fixed to the Saturday timetable.
